- Possibility: Since so many things in Jessamine default to 0, a random multiplicative operation will probably have a 0 somewhere in its operands, so that genome kind of goes nowhere.

- Possibility: Since functions of the form \(b_1 x + b_2 \sin(p_1 x)\) are not good approximations to F2, there's no way for the selection process to know that the \(x\) and \(\sin(p_1 x)\) terms are useful.
